What Will You Learn?

Inclusions

The Live Hands-On Workshop is a small-group, fully guided experience LIMITED TO ONLY 5 participants to ensure focused instruction and personalized support. Participants will learn how to formulate and create 10 different products. The workshop also includes a bonus business overview, covering basic pricing and SRP, an introduction to legal requirements and certifications, and quick marketing guidance for both online and physical selling. This is a fully all-inclusive session, complete with lunch meal, snacks, and free-flowing coffee, as well as all raw materials and tools used during the class. Empty packaging is also provided so you can take home your handmade products, neatly packed in a take-home loot bag. Participants receive guided formulation and production support, access to video modules to prepare you ahead, a trusted suppliers list, an e-book and an optional self-assessment tool paired with an e-certificate and Certificate of Attendance.

Live Face-to-Face Class

Venue & Schedule

All live hands-on workshops are conducted at Thera Kitchen’s Main Studio in Biñan, Laguna (see map link below)—a dedicated space designed for small-group, focused, and fully guided sessions. Workshop schedules are announced on our Facebook and Instagram pages, and sessions are typically held on Saturdays from 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM.

How to enroll? Can I reschedule?

Rates/Payment

The regular workshop fee is ₱5,000, with special promos and Early Bird rates occasionally announced on our Facebook and Instagram pages—be sure to watch out for these limited offers.
To keep each class fully hands-on and focused, workshops are LIMITED to only 5 participants per session. A ₱2,000 down payment is required to secure your slot, which is also used to prepare the ingredients and raw materials needed for the class. The remaining balance is paid on-site on the day of the workshop. In case of emergencies, your down payment remains valid for up to one year, provided you notify us of your intent to reschedule on or before 5 days before your chosen schedule (every Monday). Please note that failure to notify or a no-show will result in a ₱500 deduction from the down payment.
