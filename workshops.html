THERA KITCHEN's main vision is to empower the filipino community to create safe, effective, sustainable and market-ready natural organic cosmetic products—helping them to take control of the ingredients they put on their skin, live a premium eco-friendly lifestyle with confidence, save more money, or even unlock a meaningful opportunity to earn.
Our workshops provides practical and formulation-focused training designed for beginners, DIY formulators, and aspiring natural beauty entrepreneurs. The workshops go beyond recipes—covering ingredient function, safety, formulation logic, and real-world application so you can confidently create, improve, or sell your own products.
